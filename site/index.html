WORKSHOP JOURNAL · 27 OCTOBER 2026

A day of little
steps forward.

Browser update · 0.3.0 preview
Shared game snapshot: 27 October, evening

First steps, then a special delivery

A picture-led swipe and lantern lesson welcomes fresh games. The egg then floats into the grove by parachute. Unfinished lessons resume; an interrupted descent replays. Existing browser companions keep their lives.

Seven minutes to a little hello

The egg card starts at 00:07:00. Three separate care visits still matter. After hatching, keep the proposed name or swipe through letters, up to six characters. Rename later from the profile.

Care you can read on their face

Hungry, dirty, tired and frightened expressions make needs visible. A snack, a wash and a few seconds of chosen bedtime help them recover. Prolonged unattended neglect can end a life; the memorial asks for two deliberate taps before a new egg arrives.

A more comfortable handheld

Today also brought clearer pictograms, swipe menus, a quieter top bar, richer reactions, cozy bedtime animation, saved night poses, six games over two pages and the larger day/night lantern. These shared renderer and gameplay changes are included in this browser refresh.

Still on the workbench

The trained offline companion brain, full hardware performance checks and a 24-hour offline device soak remain open. A complete graphics and UI overhaul is in design review; the existing creatures stay in this build. This browser preview pauses while hidden and has no physical sensors.

Images above are actual shared-renderer simulator captures with synthetic inputs, not concept illustrations or photographs of the device. Browser progress stays on this browser.
