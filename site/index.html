ONE EGG. A FRIENDSHIP OF YOUR OWN.

Some things grow
with a little attention.

Keep your mystery egg warm. Settle it gently. When it hatches, discover Mosslet, Emberkin or Lunapip — three original species with their own silhouettes and playful expressions.

Share a snack, brush away the bubbles, or balance a leaf together. Tap the hanging lantern to turn the grove into a quiet night garden. Growth, memories and a changing home give each life its own story.

Designed for a round ESP32-S3 handheld, with a matching desktop simulator and now a browser workshop. The game’s care and artwork run locally on your device.

Hardware testing, the full offline brain and release verification are ongoing. This public preview is a place to explore the game as it grows.
